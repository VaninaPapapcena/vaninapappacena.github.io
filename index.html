About me

The strategist behind the system

I am an organizational strategist working at the intersection of DesignOps, ethics, and complexity. My work helps organizations improve how decisions are made, how teams collaborate, and how services scale under real operational pressure.

My background combines public-sector scale, service design, accessibility, and digital transformation. I led the cultural and technical adoption of a centralized design system across 50+ digital touchpoints used by more than 3 million citizens.
