About me

The strategist behind the system

I am an organizational strategist working at the intersection of DesignOps, ethics, and complexity. I help teams improve how decisions are made, how collaboration happens, and how services scale under real operational pressure.

My background combines high-scale public-sector operations, service design, accessibility, and digital transformation. I led the cultural and technical adoption of a centralized design system across 50+ digital touchpoints used by more than 3 million citizens in the Government of Buenos Aires.

  • The Operator: I led digital transformation and service standardization at scale in the Government of Buenos Aires.
  • The Thinker: My master’s work connects interactive design, ethics, and long-term decision-making.
  • The Facilitator: I support founders and teams through workshops, discovery, and strategic alignment.

Currently based in Dublin, available for remote and hybrid consulting, strategic facilitation, and fractional collaboration.
