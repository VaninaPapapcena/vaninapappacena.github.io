Selected Work · Design systems

Design-system adoption through documentation and enablement

Beyond creating components, this work focused on helping teams understand, adopt, and use shared standards in a practical way across everyday delivery.

Project summary

Help teams use the system, not just admire it

A design system only works when teams can interpret it, trust it, and integrate it into their delivery routines. This part of the work focused on documentation, shared references, and adoption support.

  • FocusDocumentation, contribution logic, and team enablement
  • UsersDesigners, developers, and multidisciplinary delivery teams
  • ValueMore consistent use of standards across products

What changed

From isolated rules to an adoptable shared language

  • Connected component guidance to real team workflows and implementation needs.
  • Reduced ambiguity around how and when to use shared patterns.
  • Supported a more sustainable relationship between standards and everyday delivery.

Outcome

Higher adoption, better continuity

Clearer references

Documentation became more useful for real product work.

Lower ambiguity

Teams had a stronger base for design and implementation decisions.

More coherence

Shared system logic became easier to sustain over time.
