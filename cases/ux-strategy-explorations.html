Selected Work · UX strategy

UX strategy explorations for clearer service journeys

Strategic interface and flow explorations designed to help teams see better journeys, organize information more clearly, and align on a stronger digital direction.

Project summary

Use visual exploration to unlock better decisions

In complex services, teams often need to see possible directions before they can align. This work translated UX thinking into tangible proposals for structure, hierarchy, and user flow.

  • FocusJourney clarity, information hierarchy, and interface direction
  • MethodExploration artefacts to support discussion and prioritization
  • ResultBetter shared understanding before implementation

Role

Frame options and make complexity visible

  • Explored ways to simplify service journeys and reduce cognitive overload.
  • Created visual direction to support alignment between strategy and execution.
  • Helped make UX decisions discussable before teams committed to delivery.

Outcome

More clarity, less guesswork

Stronger hierarchy

Information became easier to read, scan, and organize.

Sharper direction

Teams could compare options and discuss trade-offs more clearly.

Earlier alignment

Strategic exploration reduced ambiguity before implementation work started.
